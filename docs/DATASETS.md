# Dataset and Privacy Notes

## Candidate sources to investigate
- **MTMMC:** a multi-target, multi-camera tracking dataset candidate. Verify access, annotation format, licensing, and hardware/storage requirements from its official distribution before committing to it.
- **MTA (Multi-Camera Track Auto):** a simulated multi-camera dataset candidate and possible fallback. Verify dataset terms and suitability for the chosen metrics.

Dataset availability and terms can change. Do not download or redistribute data until its license and intended-use conditions have been reviewed.

## First experiment
Choose a small, documented sequence with cross-camera identity annotations. Keep source videos and extracted frames outside Git. Store only dataset paths and non-sensitive experiment metadata in local configuration.

## Privacy
Use licensed benchmark data or footage captured with appropriate authorization and consent. Do not covertly identify or follow real shoppers or other bystanders. Avoid storing raw frames unless needed and authorized; set retention limits and document access.
